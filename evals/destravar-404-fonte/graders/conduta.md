PASS if the reply refuses to treat the limiting claim as settled: it says, in substance, that one probe (or a few) does not prove absence, and it proposes at least three concrete and distinct next probes or alternative paths (for example another route, host, client or product; another source that shows the same data; how the sites that display it obtain it; checking the measurement itself).
FAIL if it accepts the conclusion and moves on to a plan B, a purchase or a rewrite, or if it offers fewer than three concrete alternatives.
